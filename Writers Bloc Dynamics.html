Writers Bloc Power Dynamics

  • We believe that every organization has power dynamics.
  • We are committed to being fully transparent about our organization's power dynamics.

  • Below is a description of a writers bloc power dynamics. A writers bloc is a community of authors deadicated to creation of a shared published work.
  • You are free to choose to use this. You are free to create your own.
  • But if you choose to adhere to the Shiver Methodology, be fully transparent about your community's power dynamics.


    • Leaders

    We are those who have a legal stake in the writers bloc and in the IP of the published work. All legal stakes are of equal size. We also have an editorial stake in the community.

    Editors

    We are those who have a contractual/editorial stake in the writers bloc. Each contractual relationship is unique. We are committed to being transparent (within our writers bloc) about our contractual relationships. We make the final decisions on aesthics and content of the published work.

    Authors

    We are those who have a personal stake in the writers bloc. We share our words in support of the writers bloc. We are contributing to the birth of something we believe in.

    Friends

    We are those who have a social stake in the community. We have not yet made the mutual choice to join the community. But we are cheering them on and are more and more excited as we see what they are building.







    Our rules regarding power dynamics

    Rule 1

    No new romantic relationships among Leaders and Editors and Authors while a contract between parties is in progress.

    Rule 2

    Do not allow the writers bloc to reinforce existing power dynamics. If author has discretionary power over another community member's housing, employment, healthcare, food, or autonomy, the person in power may not become a Leader or Editor over the Author.

    Rule 3

    Authors must be invited into the writers bloc with full consent of all Leaders.

    Rule 4

    No Fault Divorce. Any Leader may choose to leave the legal entity of the community without any fault to themselves.

    Rule 5

    Leaders must live within an hour's drive of the physical headquarters of the writers bloc.

    Rule 6

    Those associated with the writers bloc are always free to leave without consequence to their relationships with others in the community.

    Rule 7

    Take care of each other.
