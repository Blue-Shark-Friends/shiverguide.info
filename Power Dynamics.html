Power Dynamics

Every organization has power dynamics. Below is a list of simplified power dynamics in order to make clear the differences in power between community members.

You are free to choose one of these to use. You are free to create your own.

Contact us if you wish to add your unique power dynamics to this list so other communities can use them as a template to describe their own internal dynamics.

If you choose to adhere to the Shiver Methodology, be fully transparent about your community's power dynamics.
